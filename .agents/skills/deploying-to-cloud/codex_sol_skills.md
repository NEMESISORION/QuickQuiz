# Codex Sol Skills Catalog

This document contains a comprehensive list of all modular instruction sets available for dynamic loading within the Codex system.

---

## 1. Core Engineering & Systems

### `filegen-via-code`
*   **Description:** Automates the creation, transformation, and management of document formats (PDF, CSV, Excel, Word, Markdown, LaTeX) using safe Python scripts.
*   **Use Cases:** Automating report generation, bulk data export, and layout conversions.

### `dynamic-map`
*   **Description:** Renders interactive, multi-layered geographic visualizations and complex routing logic.
*   **Use Cases:** Spatial analytics, logistics plotting, and localized service heatmaps.

### `dataviz`
*   **Description:** Translates complex mathematical equations, statistical distributions, and financial milestones into clear visual models.
*   **Use Cases:** Plotting derivatives, financial forecasting, and rendering statistical graphs.

---

## 2. Industry-Specific Domains

### `finance`
*   **Description:** Handles complex corporate financial structures, tax planning models, macroeconomic data streams, and long-term asset simulations.
*   **Use Cases:** Portfolio stress-testing, cash-flow projection, and cross-border tax logic.

### `local`
*   **Description:** Processes localized datasets including business discovery, event aggregation, review sentiment, and geo-targeted service inventory.
*   **Use Cases:** Proximity recommendations, venue vetting, and localized event matching.

### `travel`
*   **Description:** Interfaces with cross-border logistics, flight routing algorithms, multi-destination itineraries, and lodging parameters.
*   **Use Cases:** Optimization of multi-leg trips, transit time reduction, and travel cost tracking.

---

## 3. Specialized Functional Contexts

### `civics`
*   **Description:** Analyzes legislative texts, voting histories, campaign finance filings, and policy positions.
*   **Use Cases:** Bill comparison, tracking regulatory changes, and evaluating public policy impacts.

### `sports`
*   **Description:** Aggregates complex player metrics, game statistics, historical contract tracking, and advanced roster simulations.
*   **Use Cases:** Draft simulation, player performance analytics, and competitive league tracking.

## 4. Digital Media & Entertainment

### `video-games`
*   **Description:** Tracks release frameworks, compatibility matrices, gaming engine constraints, and expansive narrative lore profiles.
*   **Use Cases:** Cross-platform hardware validation, interactive narrative mapping, and mechanic parsing.

### `tv-movie`
*   **Description:** Evaluates cinematic meta-properties, distribution platforms, structural narrative arcs, and industry casting datasets.
*   **Use Cases:** Content distribution analysis, narrative trope tracking, and media cataloging.

### `books`
*   **Description:** Dissects literary frameworks, publishing histories, complex bibliography linking, and deep thematic profiles.
*   **Use Cases:** Cataloging large literary databases, cross-reference mapping, and thematic analysis.

### `music`
*   **Description:** Manages audio composition structures, artist discographies, production datasets, and digital streaming metadata.
*   **Use Cases:** Audio meta-tagging, discography indexing, and structural genre classification.

## 5. Content & Evaluation Frameworks

### `creative-writing-pad`
*   **Description:** Refines, drafts, and audits high-level text architectures including templates, critical summaries, formal applications, and specialized correspondence.
*   **Use Cases:** Proposal formatting, template standardizations, and linguistic tone polishing.

### `inline-quiz`
